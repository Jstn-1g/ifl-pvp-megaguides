# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing viewpoints, constructive feedback, accountability, and attention to the health of the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery; trolling, derogatory comments, or personal and political attacks; public or private harassment; publishing another person’s private information; match harassment, doxxing, threats, or targeted abuse involving players, developers, or contributors; and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project leaders may clarify and enforce these standards, remove or reject contributions that violate them, and communicate the reason when appropriate. Reports may be made privately to `support@iflpvp.com`. Please protect the privacy and security of everyone involved.

Potential outcomes range from a private correction or warning to a temporary or permanent ban, depending on the severity and pattern of behavior.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when someone is officially representing the project in public.
